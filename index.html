■ HOW TO PLAY ■

OBJECTIVE

You are the supreme leader of a nation. Over 10-30 turns, build the most powerful empire through diplomacy, economics, military force, and disease management. Highest score wins.

TURN STRUCTURE

1️⃣
Roll the dice — result determines your action category for this turn.
2️⃣
Three action cards flip up. Each shows cost, and your odds of success.
3️⃣
Pick a card, then tap a target country on the globe.
4️⃣
Roll again for execution. Higher roll = better outcome.
5️⃣
Both AIs take their turns. Watch their arrows on the globe.

ACTION CATEGORIES

DIPLOMATIC Build alliances, send envoys, insert spies. Cheapest path to allies.

ECONOMIC Trade deals, foreign aid, sanctions. Builds your treasury fastest.

MILITARY Deploy troops, covert ops, arms deals. Raises global tension.

WILD CARD Roll a 6 and pick any action from any category.

SCORING (10 TURNS)

💰
MONEY 40% — Treasury at game end. Income scales with GDP tier and ally count.
🤝
ALLIES 30% — Every allied country adds significant points. Ally early.
🧬
DISEASE 15% — Outbreaks fire randomly. Containing them earns points and lowers tension.
🕵
OPS 10% — Successful spy and covert operations earn prestige points.
🌍
STABILITY 5% — Bonus for keeping global tension low at game end.

GLOBAL TENSION

Military actions raise tension. Diplomacy lowers it. If tension hits 100%, World War erupts and military strength determines the winner — bypassing economic scoring.

THE AI OPPONENTS

IRON FIST plays the authoritarian bloc. Prefers military actions, spikes tension fast. Watch for their red arrows.

THE OPPORTUNIST plays the neutral bloc. Copies whoever is currently winning. Dangerous late game.

PRO TIPS

⚡
Foreign Aid is cheap and has 85% success rate — great for building allies fast early.
⚡
High GDP tier countries (US, China, Japan, Norway) earn far more income per turn.
⚡
If you're losing on points late game, spike tension to 100% — a World War win gives a +100B treasury bonus.
⚡
Tap any country during the game to see its full intel and current alliance status.
